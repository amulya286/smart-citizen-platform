Smart Citizen Engagement Platform

Problem Statement

Rapid urbanization has increased civic issues such as waste accumulation, damaged roads, drainage failures and poor lighting infrastructure. Traditional complaint systems lack transparency, real-time monitoring, and citizen participation. Citizens submit complaints but remain unaware of response time, service arrival, or resolution progress. This leads to inefficient governance and reduced public trust.

Our Solution

The Smart Citizen Engagement Platform digitally connects citizens with municipal authorities through a centralized smart interface. The system enables:

  • Online civic service requests
  • Live tracking of government service vehicles
  • Automated complaint routing
  • Real-time notifications
  • Transparent resolution tracking
  • Citizen feedback-driven governance

By integrating technology with public services, the platform transforms cities into responsive, sustainable, and citizen-centric smart ecosystems.
